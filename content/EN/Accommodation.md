For your convenience, we recommend staying within the **Balneario de Archena** resort. There are three hotels directly connected to the thermal baths and the event areas, so you won't have to worry about transportation.

### 🏨 On-Site Hotels

The resort offers three options of different categories, all located just a few steps from the celebration venue:

1.  **Hotel Termas (4\*\*\*\*):** The most iconic hotel, featuring 19th-century Nasrid-style decor.
2.  **Hotel Levante (4\*\*\*\*):** A modern, functional, and very comfortable option.
3.  **Hotel León (3\*\*\*):** A practical and cozy alternative within the same grounds.

> [!terracota] Relax & Spa
> All hotels include access to the Thermal Pools. Don't forget your swimwear!

### 📅 How to Book
We are currently managing a **room block / guest discount**. 

* **Option A:** You can book directly through the [Official Balneario Website](https://balneariodearchena.com/en/hotels/).
* **Option B:** By calling (+34) 968 680 100 and mentioning that you are attending the **Wedding of Carlo and Jesús**.

> [!terracota] Book early and save
> Book more than 45 days in advance and get a 15% discount on your reservation.


### 🏠 Other options

If you prefer an experience outside the resort, there are very comfortable alternatives in both the town of Archena and the city of Murcia, which can be found on [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com) and [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com).

> [!info-boda] Important
> The spa and its hotels are located about 25 minutes by car from Murcia city center. Please keep this in mind if you prefer to book a hotel in the city. If you choose one of these alternatives and need help with transportation logistics to the resort on the wedding day, please let us know.
