For your convenience, we recommend staying within the **Balneario de Archena** resort. There are three hotels directly connected to the thermal baths and the event areas, so you won't have to worry about transportation.

### 🏨 On-Site Hotels
The resort offers three options of different categories, all located just a few steps from the celebration venue:

1.  **Hotel Termas (4****):** The most iconic hotel, featuring 19th-century Nasrid-style decor.
2.  **Hotel Levante (4****):** A modern, functional, and very comfortable option.
3.  **Hotel León (3***):** A practical and cozy alternative within the same grounds.
