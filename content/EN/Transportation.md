> [!IMPORTANT]
>Don't forget that your passport must be valid for at least 6 months to enter Spain.

For our family and friends traveling from international locations, here are the best options for reaching the Balneario de Archena.

### 🛬 Step 1: International Flight
The best option is to fly into **Madrid-Barajas Airport (MAD)**, which offers direct connections from Mexico City. 

You may also consider flying into **Alicante Airport (ALC)** if you find a convenient layover in another European capital, as it is the closest airport to the venue.


### 🚄 Step 2: From Madrid to Murcia (Recommended)
From Madrid, the most comfortable and fastest way to travel is by high-speed train (**AVE** or **Ouigo**).

* **Departure:** Madrid-Chamartín or Puerta de Atocha Station.
* **Destination:** Murcia del Carmen.
* **Duration:** Between 2h 45min and 3h 30min.
* **Tip:** Tickets usually go on sale 3 months in advance. Booking early saves a lot!
* **Booking:** [Trenes.com](https://www.trenes.com), [Omio.com](https://www.omio.com)


### 🚗 Step 3: The Final Leg (Murcia to Archena)
Once you arrive in the city of Murcia, the Balneario is approximately a **25-minute** drive away.

1. **Rental Car:** Ideal if you plan to explore the surrounding area or visit the coast.
2. **Taxi / Cabify:** There is a permanent taxi stand at the Murcia train station. The approximate cost to the Balneario is €35-45.
3. **Transfer:** If several of you arrive at the same time, we can help coordinate shared transportation.
